What it is

Smart Lead Agent 
Turns your chat conversations into summarised, categorised leads every day. 
The Smart Lead Agent reads your chat conversations once a day, writes a short summary of each, sorts it into the
  lead categories you define, and turns it into a lead your team can follow up. Nobody has to read every chat. 
What it does 
Picks up every conversation that has no summary yet. 
Writes a summary of what the customer wanted and puts it in one of 
your 
categories (for example
    “Admission enquiry” or “Wholesale order”; up to 50, in your own words). 
Creates or updates the lead, with the customer’s details from the chat, on your 
Leads 
page. 
Turn it on 
Open 
Agent & Chat → Agents 
and switch on the Smart Lead Agent. 
Choose the time it runs each day and your timezone (new companies start at 3:00,
    Asia/Dhaka). 
List your lead categories, separated by commas. 
Configure 
Run time and timezone: 
it runs once a day, after that time in your timezone. 
Categories: 
the only categories it will use. Change them any time; the next run uses the new list. 
What the customer sees 
Nothing. It works on conversations that have already happened. Your team sees the new leads with their summaries. 
Credits and limits 
Each conversation it summarises uses one 
lead agent credit 
. If the summary fails, the credit is
  given back and the conversation is tried again on the next run. Lead agent credits per month by plan:

Auto Lead Capture 
See which anonymous visitors are ready to buy, and turn them into leads. 
Auto Lead Capture watches how anonymous visitors use your website (pages, time, scrolling, forms), scores their

What it records 
Page views, time on page (idle time is not counted), how far down the page the visitor scrolled, and the first
    time they start typing in a form (not when the first field is a password). 
Each event carries the page path, title, referrer and UTM campaign tags. 
Your own events: 
caq.push(['track', 'cta_click', 1, {label: 'Book a demo'}]) 
At most 300 events per 60 seconds from one visitor.

Identify a visitor 
The chat widget identifies a visitor automatically when they give their email. Your own login or sign-up form can do
  the same:

The score 
A score from 0 to 100, worked out by rules, not AI, when a visitor is identified. Default weights, which you can change:
  page view 10, dwell time 15, scroll depth 10, cta click 25, form start 20, return visit 20. Recent
  visitors score higher than ones last seen weeks ago. When your plan includes lead capture credits, one credit also buys
  an AI summary of the visitor; it is refunded if the summary fails.
