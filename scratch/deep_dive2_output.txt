The add-on that follows up with your leads by email. 
AI Lead Engagement Agent 
is a paid add-on that works your leads by email: it sends the first follow-up,
  writes the next ones from your follow-up plan and your knowledge, reads the replies, and hands a lead to a person when
  it should. It is bought separately, on these plans: Growth, Scale, Enterprise,
  and includes 1500 engagement credits a month (one credit is one AI call). In the dashboard: 
Lead Engagement → Settings 
.

Write your follow-up plan: 
how many follow-ups, how many days apart, and what each email should
    achieve. Choose which lead sources to engage and your sending days, hours and daily cap.

Support Ticket Agent 
Raises support tickets from the chat when a customer needs a person. 
The Support Ticket Agent notices when a customer in the chat needs a person, for example a payment problem, a
  damaged order or a complaint, collects their email and the details, and raises a support ticket for your team, with
  the conversation attached. 
What it does 
Recognises when the chat agent cannot solve a problem on its own. 
Always asks for the customer’s email first, then creates the ticket with a clear subject and description. 
Tells the customer a ticket has been raised and that your team will contact them. 
Your team replies from the ticket; the customer gets the reply by email, and their email answers come back into
    the ticket (checked every 3 minutes when your mailbox is connected). 
Turn it on 
Open 
Agent & Chat → Agents 
and switch on support tickets. 
Choose where tickets go (below) and the default priority (new companies start at 
medium 
). 
Connect your support mailbox so replies are sent from your own address.

The chat asks for their email, confirms the ticket, and later they receive your team’s reply by email. 
Credits and limits 
Raising a ticket does not use extra credits; the chat messages around it use chat credits as usual.

The widget, the Chat API and the lead capture tracker answer only for origins on your allowed-domains list, and

How your data is kept apart 
Every record belongs to one company. 
Knowledge, offerings, conversations, leads, tickets,
    integrations and requests are stored with your company and every screen and API reads only your company’s
    rows. Asking for another company’s ticket, suggestion or offering by its id is answered as not found. 
Your knowledge is searched only in your own space. 
The vectors your agent searches are stored in a
    namespace of your company’s own; another company’s agent never searches it. 
Central AI’s own assistant is separate. 
The assistant on Central AI’s website has its own
    knowledge and never reads yours.
